Who can use Saltana

You must be at least 18. You are responsible for the activity on your account.

Your content

You keep ownership of what you say, post, and share. You give Saltana permission to host that content so the app can work. Do not post illegal content, threats, harassment, sexual content involving anyone under 18, or scams. We may remove content and close accounts that break these rules.

Music

Saltana lets you play audio and stream it live to other people in a room. Saltana does not choose that audio and does not check who owns it. You are responsible for making sure you have the right to play or upload it. If you do not own it, or do not have permission, do not play it.

Anyone can report an audio from the music player. Copyright notices can also be sent to support@saltana.live. We review reports and remove audio that should not be shared.

Voice rooms and chat

Other people in a room can hear you while your microphone is on. We do not save a recording of the room. You can block and report people inside the app.

Purchases

Diamonds and gold are digital items for use inside Saltana. They are not cash, and they cannot be withdrawn or transferred as money.

On Android, diamonds and gold are sold by Google Play. On iPhone, they are sold by Apple. The store charges you and handles refunds under its own rules. Saltana does not charge your card directly.

VIP is an in-app status you pay for with diamonds. It is not an auto-renewing subscription charged by Google Play or Apple.

Closing an account

You can delete your account in Settings, or email support@saltana.live. It closes immediately. Sign in again within 30 days if you want it back. After 30 days we erase the email, username, password, device identifier, and the profile name, bio, and photo. The steps are also on the Support page.

Changes

We may update these terms. The date at the top of this page is the date of the latest version. The Privacy Policy explains what we collect.
