Who can use Saltana

You must be at least 18. You are responsible for the activity on your account.

Your content

You keep ownership of what you say, post, and share. You give Saltana permission to host that content so the app can work. Do not post illegal content, threats, sexual content involving anyone under 18, or scams. We may remove content and close accounts that break these rules.

Voice rooms and chat

Other people in a room can hear you while your microphone is on. You can block and report people inside the app.

Purchases

Diamonds, gold, and VIP are digital items for use inside Saltana. On Android they are sold by Google Play. They are not cash and cannot be withdrawn as money.

Closing an account

You can delete your account in Settings. It closes immediately. Sign in again within 30 days if you want it back. After 30 days the account and personal data are permanently deleted.

Changes

We may update these terms. The date at the top of this page is the date of the latest version.
