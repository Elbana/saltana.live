Support

We read every message sent to support.

Saltana is a live voice chat app for adults. If something is wrong with your account, a room, or a purchase, write to us.

Email

support@saltana.live

Include your username and a short description of what happened.

Delete your account

Open Settings in the app and delete the account. It closes immediately. Sign in again within 30 days if you want it back. After 30 days the account and personal data are permanently deleted. You can also email us and ask for deletion.

Purchases

Diamonds and gold are digital items inside Saltana. They are not cash and cannot be withdrawn as money. On Android they are sold by Google Play. On iPhone they are sold by Apple. Ask that store for a refund. Saltana does not charge your card directly.

VIP is an in-app status you pay for with diamonds. It is not an auto-renewing subscription charged by Google Play or Apple.

Age

Saltana is for people 18 and older.
